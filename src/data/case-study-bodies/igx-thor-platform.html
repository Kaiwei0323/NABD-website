First rack-mountable, safety-capable infrastructure, designed for robotics, medical, and security workloads.

The convergence of AI and physical systems is moving fast—and out of the lab. From machines that interpret vision and sensor data in real time to systems operating under strict safety constraints, physical AI requires edge infrastructure designed for industrial-grade computing, rather than typical datacenter servers.

That's the design goal behind Inventec's Edge IGX 2U Atlas Server, built on the NVIDIA IGX Thor platform (NVIDIA IGX T7000 boardkit): a rack-mountable edge system engineered for deployment environments where performance matters, but reliability, manageability, and safety capabilities matter just as much.

A rack-mountable edge platform designed for enterprise operations

Edge AI is increasingly deployed in places that still need data center discipline: predictable thermal behavior, standard power distribution, remote management, and scalable rollouts. The Edge IGX 2U Atlas Server uses a 2U rack-mountable form factor to fit into existing enterprise infrastructure.

The system includes a Base Management Controller (BMC) for telemetry and monitoring. For high-throughput networking it integrates NVIDIA ConnectX-7 SmartNIC with 2×200 GbE connectivity for data-heavy sensor and video pipelines.

Configurable acceleration for edge workloads

The platform supports optional NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q or RTX PRO 5000 Blackwell GPUs. With iGPU plus dGPU, the system delivers up to 5,581 FP4-Sparse TFLOPs of AI compute for demanding real-time inference at the edge.

Platform foundation: NVIDIA IGX Thor and the software ecosystem

At the core is NVIDIA IGX Thor: 96GB of high-speed dGPU memory, up to four MIG instances for isolated workloads, industrial-grade temperature and vibration ratings, and a 10-year hardware lifecycle—with on-chip safety extensions and an integrated safety microcontroller for functional safety development.

NVIDIA IGX Software 2.0 includes frameworks such as NVIDIA Holoscan, Edge LLM SDK, and NVIDIA NIM microservices. NVIDIA AI Enterprise provides production-grade software support for long-term programs.

From early engagements to production readiness

Inventec is working with select partners and customers through early engagements, with multiple physical AI use cases under development across robotics, healthcare, and security—informing configurations, software integration, and deployment architectures ahead of broader rollouts.
