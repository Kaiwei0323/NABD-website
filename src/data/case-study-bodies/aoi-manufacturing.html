The shift toward smart manufacturing

Manufacturing is moving toward Industry 4.0—automation, IoT, AI, and analytics. Automated Optical Inspection (AOI) remains central to quality assurance. Integrating AI into AOI enables faster, more accurate, adaptive inspection that learns from data.

Traditional AOI uses cameras and rule-based algorithms; limitations include speed, false positives, scalability, and complex defect patterns. Machine learning and deep learning let systems adapt to new defects and improve over time.

Challenges of traditional AOI

  • Limited defect recognition
  • High false positive rates
  • Scalability and flexibility constraints
  • Underused operational data

The solution Inventec provides

By integrating an AI acceleration card and wireless LTE/5G and Wi-Fi modules into Intel-based edge devices with cameras, defect algorithms, and automation integration, manufacturers gain real-time quality monitoring and data-driven decisions on the shop floor—without a full infrastructure rip-and-replace.

Customer case I: textile fabric defect inspection

AI models in an edge box with cameras inspect broken yarn, corruption, fluffing, and dirt across fabrics including electronic glass, Kevlar, and carbon fiber.

Benefits: Stop conveyors when defects are found to save material; improve QC efficiency; reduce labor for inspection; improve detection rate from about 30% to 90%.

Customer case II: wheel rim lettering inspection

Fully automatic AI-accelerated equipment inspects wheel rims for wrong lettering, rework marks, and valve position issues.

Benefits: Fewer customer complaints, lower error costs, better overall quality with fewer misjudgments.
